How this is drawn

Each dot is a school, placed from the NIETE school-coordinates sheet. 352 of 433 schools have coordinates. The other 81, mostly middle, high and college campuses plus a handful of test schools, are counted in every number here but not drawn; 3 of them have the quiz.

A school has the quiz from the day any of its teachers first sends one to a class. A dot grows with the number of children who have taken that school's quizzes. Teachers' own self-tests are left out.

Sectors have no official outline in our data, so each area is drawn from its schools: every point belongs to the sector of its nearest school. The areas show where each sector's schools are, not an administrative boundary. Penetration is teachers who have sent a quiz divided by teachers on the sector's roll, the same measure as the SLO mastery page.
